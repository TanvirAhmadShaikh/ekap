User Manual

How to find answers and documents in the EKAP Knowledge Base.

Overview

The Knowledge Base lets you browse company documents by folder, search for specific files, and ask the built-in AI Assistant questions in plain language. Every answer the assistant gives is grounded in your organization's own documents and only draws on content you're permitted to see.

Browsing Folders

The left sidebar lists folders (often organized by department or topic). Click a folder to see the documents inside it. Folders can contain sub-folders — click to expand them.

Searching Documents

Use the search bar at the top of the page to find documents by title, tag, or content. Results update as you type.

Previewing & Downloading

Click any document to open a preview without leaving the page. Use the Download button in the preview to save a copy locally.

Asking the AI Assistant

Click 💬 AI Chat in the top-right to open the assistant panel. Type a question in plain language — for example:

"What are the onboarding steps?"
"Summarise recent engineering docs"
"What policies are available?"

The assistant searches across documents you have access to and answers using only that content. Use ↺ New conversation to start fresh, or ✕ to close the panel.

Understanding Citations

Every answer includes citations so you can verify it: the source document name, section, page number, a confidence score, and a link straight to that source. If a citation's confidence looks low, open the source document yourself to confirm before relying on the answer.

Why Can't I See a Document?

Documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. If a document or an answer that would reference it doesn't appear for you, it's most likely outside your role or department's permissions — the assistant will never partially reveal content you're not authorized to see. If you believe you should have access, contact your knowledge manager or system administrator.

Tips

  • Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation — the assistant keeps context.
  • Prefer specific questions ("What's the parental leave policy for engineers?") over vague ones for more precise citations.
  • If an answer seems wrong or outdated, leave feedback so it can be reviewed.
